### 5.6 What the earlier experiments can still establish

The historical numerical study remains informative when its representation boundary is explicit. In 33 selected pairs from 16 groups, source-coded rules and fields reproduce all 33 encoded local states. Automatic rule parsing with reference fields matches 30, while automatic fields with either rule representation match 17. Removing the largest family, which supplies 15 pairs, leaves 18, 15, nine and nine matches respectively. The representation loss therefore persists after that exclusion, although its magnitude is specific to the chosen cases. The later 24/33 repair is a development replay on inspected material and supplies no held-out improvement estimate.

The local executor's 466/480 correct constructed scenario slots demonstrate arithmetic and relation execution with standardized fields and other requirements satisfied by construction. They do not establish complete applicability. Of 23 real-case local numeric passes, only five have historically supported whole-description evidence; eighteen remain unknown because other conditions are unresolved. The old automatic guard corrected no binary administrative-disagreement error. For the five historically refuted cases, it reduced Qwen4's correct native refutations from three to one by converting two to unknown. Inspection identifies the policy failure: an unparsed numeric rule was allowed to override a negative answer, even when the source audit could establish a mandatory contradiction. Preserving verified refutations is a logical requirement; accepting every unverified model negative is not.

Administrative comparisons are retained in the supplement rather than promoted to the main evidence-task ranking. A source-only majority diagnostic obtains 69.1% balanced accuracy and Granite 71.7%; their paired difference of 2.6 percentage points has a wide group-resampling interval of −18.4 to 19.4. This demonstrates an available source signal, without establishing that Granite internally used that shortcut. Granite's full-input gains over facts only and rules only are 16.3 and 22.8 points, with intervals of 1.4–31.6 and 10.2–37.2. Both input types matter to that fixed implementation. Aggregate input dependence still does not verify the condition reasoning. The historical tasks, reference policies, intervals, interface failures and hypothetical costs are preserved as conditional diagnostics, not pooled with the new controlled endpoints.
