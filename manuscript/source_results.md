## 5 Results

### 5.1 Reconstructing the condition evidence

The 208 historical units divide into 132 direct, 45 interpretive, 19 insufficient and 12 disputed records. Two direct units are nonbinding: an illustrative list and a permission expressed as “whether or not.” The direct scorable core therefore contains 130 units. This stratification changes what can be evaluated by literal attributes and simple relations. It prevents a dispute about the meaning of a legal category from being scored as if it were an incorrectly compared number.

Figure 2 separates the layer assignment from the state under each admitted-input version. The strict excerpt policy yields 125 supported, five refuted and 76 unknown conditions, with two not applicable. Direct source restoration yields 129 supported, five refuted and 72 unknown. The four changes occur in three cases: connector absence in H330938, four wheels in H322923, and a self-contained motor and a 1,500 W ceiling in H340899. The motor relation had previously been inferred as supported in the historical coding; the other three relations had been historically unknown. The restoration count thus depends on the reference policy against which it is compared.

These counts describe the retained units, not a population prevalence or an expert-validated legal accuracy estimate.

All four restored relations are useful local corrections, but none alone resolves its whole case. Restricting aggregation to the 58 safe outer conjunctions gives ten supported, three refuted and 45 unknown cases, unchanged by direct restoration. The remaining 15 pairs are logic unresolved and are not included in these three-state totals. Compared with the historical 21/5/47 coding, five supported cases become strict unknown and six require a richer logic representation; two historical refutations also fall outside the simple aggregator. These changes reflect a stricter representation and admission policy. They are not evidence that the administrative outcomes were wrong.

Independent verification located all retained rule quotations, checked the raw-snapshot hashes for 67 rulings and validated 455 continuous evidence spans. Two joined product excerpts receive separate original spans rather than a fictitious continuous locator. These checks establish the provenance needed to inspect the audit. They leave the semantic mapping open to criticism and revision.

### 5.2 What the historical unknowns conceal

The 47 historically unknown pairs contain 137 condition units, of which 59 were historically unknown and 67 are unknown under the strict excerpt policy. Table 2 attributes the strict unknowns to source and interpretation problems. The sets overlap: the same case can have a clipped attribute, an unresolved definition and a component-scope problem. The conditions in this table therefore do not partition either the cases or the 67 strict unknowns.

Table 2 Overlapping causes within the 47 historically unknown pairs

| Source of unresolved evidence | Cases containing the cause / 47 | Affected strict-unknown condition units |
|---|---:|---:|
| Descriptive information omitted from the excerpt | 5 | 7 |
| No sufficient independent attribute located in the complete public source | 10 | 12 |
| Definition, category or use mapping | 36 | 38 |
| Object, component or measurement scope | 11 | 12 |
| Source conflict or competing interpretation | 5 | 5 |

The omission set is broader than the directly repaired set. H341677’s wireless condition still requires a declared meaning of “wireless enabled.” In H314080, the width and areal-mass intervals repeat the protesting party’s proposed exclusion description; they remain attributed claims with an explicit acceptance option. Party attribution alone is not an exclusion rule, as the submitted-document assertion admitted in H330938 demonstrates. Only four condition relations meet the stated direct-restoration policy. Conversely, “no sufficient attribute located” means that this public ruling does not supply the required independent assertion; it does not mean the technical document or physical fact cannot exist elsewhere.

The largest case set concerns definitions, categories or use. Its 36 cases show that generic requests for more specifications would often target the wrong problem in this collection. Information requests need an action type. A missing mass can be requested from a product dossier; an incorporated definition must be retrieved and interpreted; a conflict between a laboratory report and a party's submission requires a source-priority decision. Returning unknown without this distinction does not explain how review could proceed.

### 5.3 Interpretation changes conclusions without adding new measurements

We recomputed the 58 safe conjunctions under three fully specified interpretation packages. The strict package withholds unresolved mappings. A technical-scope package admits the listed monopolar-system and conducting-core readings, selected use and material mappings, laboratory priority for glove composition and peak output for a motor. An alternative package changes material and use scope, upright orientation, party-versus-laboratory priority and nominal-versus-peak output. Unreported attributes and interpretations without a recorded option remain unresolved. Neither package is selected to maximize agreement with an administrative result.

Table 3 Joint interpretation-policy sensitivity in the 58 safe conjunctions

| Declared package | Supported | Refuted | Unknown |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Withhold unresolved interpretations | 10 | 3 | 45 |
| Listed technical scope, laboratory priority and peak output | 17 | 8 | 33 |
| Listed alternative material/use scope, party priority and nominal output | 19 | 5 | 34 |

These simultaneous results show how admission and interpretation can change the object being scored. They are diagnostic packages rather than authoritative legal doctrines. Separate per-case enumeration gives marginal possible-count envelopes of 10–20 supported, 3–9 refuted and 33–45 unknown. These are not three jointly attainable ranges, probabilities or bounds over every reasonable interpretation. Their purpose is to identify precisely which recorded mapping changes a result. H330938, for example, can be supported, refuted or unknown under different declared material and topology choices after its connector fact is restored.

The inventory analysis supplies structural context for those choices. All 397 entries were read under a common annotation scheme. The 354 product descriptions contain 1,096 source-anchored coarse groups; the 43 code-only entries add no physical product groups but retain their classification and date gates. Component dimensions, alternative outputs, lists and conditional guards remain inside their declared groups. Thirteen entries are explicitly marked unresolved under this reading. A source wording anomaly, such as a shelf width printed as 183 m, is preserved rather than silently corrected. The resulting records support inspection of rule structure; they do not establish a prevalence of difficult transactions or a complete executable ontology. Only 16 of the 397 index entries have normalized exact-description matches in the case collection, which further limits coverage claims.
