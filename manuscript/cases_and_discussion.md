## 6 Five source-to-condition case chains

### 6.1 Connector absence: a gap created by input selection

[H330938](https://rulings.cbp.gov/ruling/H330938) concerns two cable descriptions. The candidate requires monopolar conductors, voltage above 1,000 V, a material restriction expressed as “other than of copper,” and absence of connectors. The frozen input contains an aluminum conducting core and an explicit 2,000 V wiring rating. It omits the decisive connector description. The complete ruling reports that the submitted documentation describes the cables as imported on reels at long lengths without connectors. This is an independently identifiable descriptive assertion in the agency's analysis. Its location outside the retained excerpt explains the earlier unknown connector state: the gap was introduced by input selection.

Restoring this assertion changes the connector condition from unknown to supported under the direct-text policy. It does not settle the entire description. Whether the cable's single-pole system establishes the intended monopolar category remains a mapping question. The aluminum core and copper shield also require a decision about which component the copper exclusion governs. Restricting the predicate to the conducting core supports it; extending it to every copper-bearing part refutes it. The audit retains those policies separately instead of attributing both uncertainties to missing measurements. The resulting chain identifies one repairable input omission and two remaining interpretation choices. An information request for connector status is unnecessary once the public source assertion has been admitted; a whole-case affirmative answer is still not licensed by that repair alone.

### 6.2 A jack's dimensions and its lifting capacity are different predicates

[H318593](https://rulings.cbp.gov/ruling/H318593) describes a three-ton floor jack. Against the first candidate description, its stated dimensions of 61 × 30.2 × 15.2 cm appear comfortably below ceilings of 81 × 41 × 25 cm, and its mass of 26.4 kg is below 52 kg. The dimensional statement expressly excludes the handle. Establishing the dimensions of the complete jack therefore requires matching the measurement scope before comparing values. The source audit retains the mass comparison as supported and the complete-dimension condition as unknown. The description of 68.50% steel with aluminum components introduces a separate category question about “of steel.” Review needs complete dimensions and an explicit mixed-metal interpretation.

A second candidate in the same ruling limits lifting capacity to 550 kg. The stated capacity of 6,000 lb converts to 2,721.55422 kg and directly refutes that necessary condition. This value concerns the supported load, rather than the jack's own mass. It remains decisive even if a different attribute is unreported. The pair demonstrates two distinct audit actions: seek or define the correct measurement scope for the first candidate, and preserve an established contradiction for the second. A guard that converts every unparsed numeric description into overall unknown can erase the latter finding. The problem is the relation and the fusion policy, rather than conjunction itself.

### 6.3 Relay ratings require both product and operating-role binding

[N311039](https://rulings.cbp.gov/ruling/N311039) describes four power relays. The candidate voltage condition is greater than 60 V and no more than 250 V; contacts must also be rated at least 10 A. The four products have coil/contact voltage pairs of 12/277, 110/277, 48/277 and 48/277 V. Their contact-current ratings are 30, 40, 40 and 40 A. Each number retains its product and rating role. A pooled interval spanning all reported voltages would insert values that the source never reports and discard which rating belongs to which relay.

The second product is role dependent: its 110 V coil lies inside the range while its 277 V contacts do not. Products one, three and four fail the voltage limit under either listed rating role, because their coil voltages are too low and their contact voltages too high. Under the declared all-listed-products task, the voltage condition is therefore refuted even without choosing a role for the second product. The current threshold is independently supported. This updates an earlier AI coding that treated the electrical information as unresolved. A determinate conclusion about the quantified set survives the remaining ambiguity because other members fail every represented alternative.

### 6.4 Glove composition: the value is present, but its authority is contested

[H333371](https://rulings.cbp.gov/ruling/H333371) concerns polyurethane-coated gloves. The material condition requires less than 50% by weight of textile fibers. The public record contains both a party-supplied composition of 48% polyester and a reported CBP laboratory result of approximately 76% textile. The product description, plastic coating and enhanced-grip purpose have direct support. The unresolved condition is the textile fraction and which assertion is admitted as controlling. This is not an absent-number problem.

Under laboratory priority, the textile condition is refuted; under party priority, it is supported. A strict record that declines to resolve the conflicting assertions leaves it unknown. These are explicit alternative policies, not equally authoritative legal recommendations. Laboratory evidence may have compelling institutional priority, but that priority is an argument to state and evaluate rather than an invisible preprocessing decision. Appending another copy of either number cannot reconcile the conflict. A prospective interface would need to expose the competing reports, their attribution and a rule for resolving them. The short condition-output schema used in the controlled demonstration cannot express that full conflict and is not claimed to do so.

### 6.5 A passive-infrared subsystem within a multisensor apparatus

[H333673](https://rulings.cbp.gov/ruling/H333673) concerns a rain–light–solar–humidity sensor assembly. The public source distinguishes several sensing functions and describes the front-light sensor as using passive infrared detection for light switching. It also describes other sensors in the assembly operating on different principles. The candidate describes apparatus using passive infrared detection sensors and designed to turn lights on and off. The light-control function has direct textual support. The infrared condition raises a scope question: does use by the relevant light-control subsystem suffice, or must a broader apparatus category be established?

Neither the existence of other sensor types nor the word “apparatus” by itself establishes a universal requirement that every component use passive infrared. Conversely, one passive-infrared component does not automatically settle the complete legal category. We preserve the disputed scope and withhold this case from a flat necessary-conjunction conclusion. This treatment shows why the 15 logic-unresolved pairs are not simply difficult unknown examples for the same aggregator. Their representation needs further work. The appropriate next action concerns a category or quantifier interpretation, rather than a generic request to provide an infrared number.

## 7 Discussion

### 7.1 Evidence construction changes the object being evaluated

The source audit identifies three operations with different implications. Restoring a descriptive assertion repairs an omission in benchmark input construction. Declaring a material, category or measurement mapping resolves a policy choice, conditionally. Correcting a product or component binding changes which relation is being tested. Collapsing these operations into a single “insufficient evidence” class obscures both the origin of uncertainty and the action that could address it.

This distinction also changes the interpretation of an apparent model error. A model that agrees with a complete administrative ruling may be unsupported by the shortened input it receives. A model that asks for a fact omitted by the benchmark may be correctly responding to that input, while appearing unhelpful in a public-source audit where the fact is already available. The evaluation should specify its reference object before scoring: agreement with an administrative disposition, support in an admitted text, or validity under an authoritative legal interpretation. These are related but different endpoints.

The new evidence records make the admitted-text endpoint reproducible and expose choices relevant to legal interpretation. A reader can localize a disagreement to a quote, target object, rating role, admission policy or logical scope and recompute its consequence. The resource thereby supports a substantive challenge to a label, including challenges that agreement between annotators would conceal.

### 7.2 A bounded role for structured assistance

A supplied condition list makes the reported basis of a decision more inspectable. It allows a state to be connected to a numbered fact and an unresolved requirement to a specific request. This is a richer output contract than a whole-case label. Its value should be judged through whether those additional outputs are correct and useful, including whether the system stops requesting information after a decisive contradiction. High decision accuracy alone cannot establish that benefit.

The functional demonstration supports a limited implementation claim: explicit evidence changes can be evaluated through state, citation, request and relation endpoints in one reproducible protocol. The checklist matched six more whole-input references in the controlled collection, while matching one fewer endpoint in the separate source-restoration probe. Both interfaces failed to update the self-contained-motor condition appropriately. Additional fields exposed the checklist's unsupported refutation and missing request, but did not prevent them. These results make the quality of each intermediate operation assessable; they do not establish uniform improvement from structured output.

Automatic legal-rule construction remains outside this demonstration. The inventory makes that boundary concrete. It contains code-only gates, date-dependent code transitions, alternatives, incorporated standards and constraints tied to different components. A complete parser would need to retain those relations and account for unresolved source wording. The present audit supplies a representation and test cases through which such a parser could be assessed; it does not claim that providing two controlled conditions has solved that problem.

### 7.3 Transfer, cost and limits of practical inference

The transferable object is the audit protocol and its record schema. Licensing, tax relief, product regulation and procurement decisions also combine descriptive assertions, party submissions, incorporated definitions and applicability reasoning. Transfer would require a new source-role policy, domain-specific mappings and validation of the target rules. The ordinary-attribute qualification cases and customs-derived controlled clauses show that the software can be exercised independently of one complete ruling. They do not estimate accuracy in those other domains.

Deployment costs include constructing a trustworthy condition list, maintaining dated rules, checking attributes and managing unresolved interpretations. Local inference makes the computational component observable, but it does not measure a professional's review time, the availability of product dossiers or the consequences of an erroneous filing. No study participant, importer workflow or field intervention is observed. We therefore report actual compute and diagnostic behavior without translating them into staff savings, duty recovery or a production recommendation. Hypothetical loss calculations in the supplement explicitly compare a review-all baseline and do not establish economic value.

The main validity limit is independent domain validation. The public source quotations are inspectable and machine-checked, but semantic assignments and interpretation packages remain AI-assisted judgments. The frame-species erratum shows that deleting one explicit statement need not remove semantic support for its condition: the remaining teak-frame statement still establishes that the frame is wooden. Public decisions are also retrospective, selectively published and purposively retrieved. They cannot establish the distribution of incomplete importer dossiers. Shared rulings and rule groups limit independent information, while the twelve constructed families share a design template. These limitations constrain claims about legal accuracy, population rates and operational benefit; they do not invalidate the narrower findings about the retained sources and frozen interfaces.

## 8 Conclusion

An evidence benchmark built from a public legal decision should preserve the path from the complete source to the admitted input and the condition it is intended to establish. In this tariff-exclusion audit, some apparent gaps arise from clipped descriptive statements, others from absent attributes, and others from interpretation or binding. These distinctions produce different reference states and different next actions. Source restoration repairs particular relations while leaving category and scope choices visible.

The accompanying records, alternative input versions, interpretation packages and controlled diagnostics support inspection and recalculation of those choices. The result is an auditable evaluation resource for a bounded legal module. Further work can add expert adjudication, richer conflict representations, automatic rule construction and prospective dossiers against that explicit baseline, rather than treating an administrative disposition or agreement among AI coders as the endpoint of validation.
