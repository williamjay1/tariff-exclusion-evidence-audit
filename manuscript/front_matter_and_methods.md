# Auditing the construction of evidence in public tariff exclusion decisions

## 1 Introduction

In a text-based evaluation of legal applicability, an answer depends on which source statements are admitted to the model input. Public administrative decisions make this dependency difficult to inspect. They contain product descriptions, submissions by interested parties, technical definitions, findings, reasoning and outcomes in the same document. A benchmark developer may retain a factual section and remove the holding to prevent answer leakage. Yet a descriptive statement in an analytical section can be lost at the same time. The resulting task then asks whether a shortened input supports an answer, while the reference answer may still come from the complete decision. A model can agree with the recorded outcome without establishing the required conditions, and it can correctly identify an information gap that the benchmark itself created.

Tariff exclusion applicability provides a concrete setting in which to examine this problem. A product may have a known tariff classification and still require assessment against an exclusion's published product description. Relevant distinctions include whether a cable has connectors, whether a capacity refers to lifting or collection, whether a material restriction concerns a conducting core or a shield, and whether different ratings belong to different products or operating roles. These are relations between a condition and its evidence. They are poorly represented by a single administrative eligibility label or an unstructured list of numbers.

The legal module is real but bounded. USTR states that the scope of an exclusion is governed by the published tariff code and product description and is available to qualifying products beyond those of the original requester (USTR 2018). We therefore study review after a candidate exclusion and a compatible base classification have been supplied. We do not attempt to classify arbitrary goods, establish origin, verify entry dates or calculate a complete duty obligation. The immediate prospective use would be an analyst checking whether a product dossier supports each requirement and what further information is needed. Our actual observations are retrospective public rulings; they allow investigation of evidence construction, rather than validation on first submissions from importers.

Existing research already provides the main computational ingredients. ContractNLI distinguishes entailment, contradiction and unmentioned information while identifying evidence spans (Koreeda and Manning 2021). Holzenberger and Van Durme (2023) connect legal information extraction to symbolic statutory reasoning and compare predicted with partially supplied knowledge bases. Medvedeva and McBride (2023) explain why facts selected in completed judgments need not represent the information available for a prospective task. Recent tax and legal discrepancy evaluations further establish the value of verifiable questions and controlled perturbations (Gogani-Khiabani et al. 2025; Roy Choudhury et al. 2026). Three-state labels, symbolic conjunction, evidence references and perturbation testing are consequently starting points here, rather than claims of methodological novelty.

The contribution is a source-to-input audit that makes the construction of a public-decision evaluation inspectable. For each historical condition unit, it records the governed product or component, signed requirement, logical role, quantifier, measurement basis, source quotation, admission policy and unresolved interpretation. It distinguishes a fact omitted from the model input from an attribute absent from the complete public source, and distinguishes both from a definition or binding problem. A second input version restores independently descriptive attributes without importing the agency's applicability conclusion. Changes can then be recomputed at the condition level, with whole-case aggregation restricted to suitable logical structures.

We apply this protocol to a purposive collection of 73 product–exclusion pairs, 208 historical condition units and 67 underlying CBP rulings. The audit revisits every previously unknown case and supplies source locators for all retained condition records. A separate inventory analysis examines the structure and coverage of a 397-entry USTR index, without treating that dated index as the historical sampling frame. Finally, a qualified local language-model interface is tested on source-anchored, explicitly constructed evidence changes. The endpoints concern missing-condition localization, unsupported affirmations, unnecessary requests, correct evidence binding and correct updates. Returning unknown at both endpoints earns no credit when the specified transformation requires the answer to change.

The central inference is about evaluation validity: an evidence label is conditional on a source version, a selection of statements and an interpretation policy. Keeping those dependencies visible creates a reusable object for criticism and recalculation. It does not establish independent legal truth through agreement between AI annotators. The article accordingly presents an evidence resource and an evaluation protocol, with a functional demonstration, rather than a new autonomous customs decision system.

## 2 Legal module and related evaluation

### 2.1 Product scope after classification

The module takes a target product description, a supplied base tariff classification and a candidate exclusion description. It asks which product requirements are established, contradicted or unresolved by the admitted text. Candidate retrieval, temporal validity and classification are upstream inputs. The output is a condition audit and, where a defensible logical representation is available, a conclusion based on the admitted input, accompanied by a specific information request when needed. It is not a recommendation to file an entry or claim a refund. A classification error or an expired exclusion cannot be repaired by satisfying the product-description conditions.

This distinction matters when relating the work to customs AI. Explainable product classification and recent hierarchical or knowledge-graph-assisted tariff benchmarks examine assignment of codes and the rules used in that assignment (Lee et al. 2024; Lan et al. 2026; Xia et al. 2026). Our candidate code is supplied; a compatible code does not settle a narrower description within that code. Conversely, the present diagnostics cannot be interpreted as evidence that upstream classification has been solved. The interface between the modules must retain the exact tariff level and historical version used in a ruling.

Institutional scale does not supply the missing task-level evidence. GAO reports approximately 53,000 requests for tariff exclusions during 2018–2020 (GAO 2021). Those requests concern establishment of exclusions, rather than the number of later transactions assessed against an already published description. We use this material to describe the institution, not to estimate the frequency, monetary exposure or labor cost of the module studied here. No transaction distribution or importer workflow is observed in our data.

### 2.2 What the closest evaluations already establish

ContractNLI is a particularly close antecedent because its task combines three-state inference and evidence identification. Its 607 nondisclosure agreements and 17 hypotheses also show that evidence may be discontinuous and that exceptions complicate document-level entailment (Koreeda and Manning 2021). Our distinction is not the addition of an unknown class. It is the audit of how a public decision becomes an input, including the provenance and statement role of information outside a section selected as factual.

Holzenberger and Van Durme (2023) provide the closest extraction–reasoning comparison. Their statutory tax task represents entities, events and attributes in a knowledge base consumed by a Prolog reasoner. Predicted, partially supplied and empty knowledge-base conditions help locate representation losses. They also discuss ambiguity and revisions to selected cases. This work precludes a claim that separating extraction from execution, or binding values to entities, is itself new. Our extension concerns admission and attribution in an administrative source: whether an attribute was supplied, clipped, definition dependent or disputed before a reasoner receives it.

Medvedeva and McBride (2023) distinguish retrospective judgment classification from a genuine future-outcome task. Removing the holding does not reverse the selection of facts by the decision writer. Our source restoration addresses a narrower problem within retrospective materials. It can repair a lost descriptive assertion; it cannot reconstruct the original dossier or remove the writer's knowledge of the outcome. We retain this boundary in both the task name and the evaluation claims.

The VITA study uses 130 IRS questions and 260 answer-preserving variants, with expert construction of answer references, to evaluate assistance with tax returns (Gogani-Khiabani et al. 2025). It provides a relevant comparison within Artificial Intelligence and Law, but its expert answer process is stronger than the legal validation available here. CLAUSE studies discrepancies and controlled legal-text perturbations, including omissions, terminology and structural changes (Roy Choudhury et al. 2026). TriBench-Ko organizes judicial risks across tasks and includes attorney review of source-derived materials; it remains a preprint in the version examined here (Lee et al. 2026). We do not claim to introduce legal omission testing or risk-oriented evaluation. We contribute source-role records and alternative admitted-input versions through which an apparent information deficit can be attributed and challenged.

Behavioral testing supplies the design principle for the functional demonstration (Ribeiro et al. 2020). We specify both transformations that should preserve a correct answer and transformations that should change it. The structured interface is supplied with conditions and therefore does not test automatic recovery of a complete legal rule. Its additional outputs make particular failures observable: an answer can be correct while its request is missing, or its cited fact can belong to another product. These failures remain visible when overall accuracy is high.

## 3 Sources and evidence audit

### 3.1 Collection and units of analysis

The historical discovery process retained 2,482 retrieved documents, produced 102 candidate records, constructed 86 product–exclusion pairs and retained 73 pairs for a compatible-code analysis. This was iterative, purposive AI-assisted discovery. The retained artifacts do not contain a complete contemporaneous screening log for every retrieved item; in particular, the earlier reduction to the candidate collection cannot be reconstructed as an exhaustive eligibility screen. The 2,482 documents are not 2,482 confirmed applicability decisions. We preserve the retrieval and selection records but make no claim about recall or inclusion probabilities.

The primary 73 pairs come from 67 rulings and 41 linked rule groups, using the retained public snapshots retrieved on 7 October 2026. Twenty-seven pairs are HQ rulings and 46 are NY rulings. Several pairs can share a ruling or exclusion family. These dependencies differ from the 208 historical condition units, the 397 index entries and the constructed diagnostic endpoints. Table 1 gives the units and their permitted uses. Repeated products, shared clauses and reverse intervention pairs are not counted as additional independent observations.

The compatible-code subset permits compatible tariff levels; a stricter exact-string subset contains 60 pairs. The 13 removed pairs all come from HQ, with two eligible and eleven ineligible administrative outcomes; all 46 NY pairs remain. Each removed pair has an eight-digit product code compared with a ten-digit candidate statistical code. We do not infer historical ten-digit classification certainty from these strings. The stricter filter changes source, class and rule-group composition together and is used only as a documented sensitivity.

For every pair, the applicable rule text is the candidate exclusion quoted in that ruling. It is not replaced with the wording of the 2024 index. The index contains 41 single-code entries, two date-dependent code transitions and 354 product descriptions. An AI-assisted semantic reading selects 1,096 coarse requirement groups within the product descriptions, preserving local alternatives, exceptions and component lists inside their groups. This is a declared representation granularity, rather than a count of legally atomic conditions. Normalized exact description matching links 21 of the 86 pairs to 16 index entries; 16 of those pairs belong to the 73-pair compatible-code audit. Matching establishes a conservative textual intersection, not temporal equivalence or complete historical coverage. Unconfirmed similar clauses remain candidates rather than matches.

### 3.2 Statement roles and source admission

Figure 1 presents the protocol. The complete retained official text is the source; the model input is a versioned selection from it. We distinguish descriptive attributes attributed to a party, laboratory findings, agency-stated product attributes, definitions or category explanations, application reasoning and the final outcome. A section heading is not an admission rule. A sentence in an analytical section may contain a separable description of a connector, motor or wheel count. Conversely, a sentence in a factual section may merely repeat a party's proposed legal characterization.

![Figure 1](../artwork/figures/figure1_source_protocol.png)

Figure 1 Source-to-input evidence audit. Descriptive assertions retain statement class and provenance. Interpretations, application reasoning and outcomes are recorded separately. The protocol represents the condition–object–attribute relationship before computing a state or an information request. Whole-case conjunction is used only where the audit represents the conditions as jointly necessary under the stated interpretation policy; unresolved alternatives and exceptions require their own representation.

The frozen excerpt is preserved unchanged. A restored version appends only descriptive attributes admitted under the stated direct-text policy. Exact character offsets and paragraph identifiers locate the original assertion. Where a retained quotation joins nonadjacent product descriptions, separate continuous spans locate its members; the joined excerpt is not presented as a continuous source quotation. We retain submitted-document attribution even when the agency reports it in its reasoning. Such provenance establishes that the public text contains the assertion, not that the physical property has been independently measured.

An attributed assertion that is contested or is itself a restatement of the requested exclusion is not silently promoted to a verified attribute. The record retains its source and the policy needed to use it. Definitions and conclusions can be consulted to identify an interpretation problem, but they are not inserted into an attribute-only input as evidence that the product satisfies the rule. This avoids the equally problematic alternatives of discarding every analytical sentence and importing the full legal answer.

### 3.3 Uniform condition records and reference layers

Each of the 208 records contains the literal rule quote; product or component; signed predicate; attribute; logical role; quantifier and scope; unit, rating role or measurement basis where relevant; factual quote and locator; historical coding; direct-text excerpt and restored states; inference basis; unknown cause; and explicit interpretation options. The historical units are not asserted to be atomic or complete. A unit can contain local alternatives, an illustrative list or a nested exception, which is recorded rather than flattened into positive requirements.

We use supported, refuted and unknown for textual evidence under a stated admission and interpretation policy. A negative requirement is treated as a signed predicate: explicit connector absence supports “not fitted with connectors,” explicit connector presence refutes it, and silence leaves it unknown. Invalid output is an interface status, never a fourth form of legal uncertainty. A nonbinding example or permission is marked not applicable for mandatory-condition scoring.

The audit has four reference layers. Direct records concern an explicitly stated attribute with a simple comparison, unit conversion or discrete membership check. Interpretive records require a technical definition, product category, functional equivalence or scope mapping beyond that direct comparison. Insufficient records lack an admitted attribute in the relevant input, with the source search determining whether the gap can be repaired. Disputed records retain conflicting assertions or unresolved object, quantifier or definition choices. Layer assignment concerns the frozen input and is preserved even when a newly admitted direct assertion changes a restored condition state.

Unknown is accompanied by a cause. Missing product attributes, omitted source statements, unavailable incorporated definitions, unresolved categories, object or measurement-scope mismatches, conflicting sources and disputed annotations call for different actions. A missing wheel count can motivate a factual request. A dispute about whether a backpack vacuum belongs to the legal class of upright cleaners cannot be resolved merely by requesting a number. Causes may overlap within a case; their counts do not partition the 47 historically unknown pairs.

For safe mandatory conjunctions, a verified refutation determines refutation, all supported conditions determine support, and the remaining combinations are unknown. This rule concerns the evidence states of genuine necessary conjuncts. It does not justify accepting an unverified negative model answer, and it does not convert a local OR into AND. Fifteen pairs are withheld from this simple aggregator because their historical units contain alternatives, exceptions, illustrative lists or unresolved composite-object scope. Condition-level records remain available for those pairs.

### 3.4 AI annotation and limits of validation

AI assistants performed the new source coding, parallel semantic review, and separate provenance and arithmetic checks. The session used the GPT-6 family through Codex; an immutable provider model-snapshot identifier was not exposed. The auditors had access to earlier project materials and administrative outcomes. This was an outcome-exposed AI audit, without independent human or domain-expert validation. The administrative disposition was excluded as a criterion for accepting a textual evidence state.

The historical two-round AI coding and its agreement statistics are retained in the supplement. Shared model lineage and prior project exposure allow shared mistakes, as the relay example demonstrates. Agreement measures repeatability of those coding procedures, not independent legal validity. The new records instead expose quotes, attribute relations and mapping alternatives so that a reader can inspect or replace them. Mechanical validation checks source hashes, literal spans, coverage and aggregation; it cannot validate a category interpretation.

The administrative disposition y remains an auxiliary historical observation. The evidence state z concerns the admitted input. The old reference contained 21 supported, five refuted and 47 unknown pairs. Its determinate states never opposed the administrative disposition: the eligible row contained 21 supported and 26 unknown cases, and the ineligible row five refuted and 21 unknown cases. It principally measured confirmation or withholding under a conservative evidence policy. We retain that cross-tabulation to prevent the old z labels from being mistaken for an independently validated legal truth.

## 4 Functional evaluation

### 4.1 Qualification and frozen interface

Four independent development cases check the implementation. Twenty-four separate qualification cases contain 27 conditions across ordinary attributes, bounds, intervals, explicit negation, object binding, measurement basis and conjunction. They do not use the formal customs materials. Before qualification, the decision gate was fixed at at least 23/24 correct decisions, no invalid or truncated responses, no unsupported affirmative decisions and correct handling of a decisive refutation alongside another missing requirement. The checklist arm additionally required at least 95% condition accuracy. Missing-request recall was measured but was not a qualification threshold.

The fixed model order was Qwen3-14B followed by Granite-3.3-8B if necessary. Qwen3-14B passed in the first qualified configuration, so the fallback was not selected. Passing these checks establishes basic interface function on the qualification cases, without establishing customs expertise or ruling out task-specific failures on the formal inputs. Every development and qualification attempt is retained, including request errors. The qualification inputs, prompts, schemas and thresholds are versioned separately from the formal collection.

Inference uses an existing officially hash-checked Q4_K_M GGUF checkpoint, a local llama.cpp server, native chat templates, disabled thinking, temperature zero, seed 20261023, a 4,096-token context and six CPU threads. No API judge supplies reference answers. The output grammar constrains JSON structure and allowed identifiers, not the factual states, cited evidence, complete condition coverage or requests. The parser and evaluator separately record missing or duplicate IDs, incomplete coverage, absent evidence for a determinate state, unsupported citations and generation limits. The supplement provides exact software and checkpoint manifests, prompts, raw responses and elapsed compute times.

### 4.2 Common semantic input and controlled changes

The direct arm receives the target, complete controlled clause and numbered facts and returns a native three-state decision. The checklist arm receives the same semantic content plus a structured duplicate of the two requirements and their IDs. It returns per-condition states, cited fact IDs, an overall decision and requested unknown-condition IDs. This is a supplied-checklist diagnostic. Neither arm is credited with discovering a complete exclusion rule, and the contrast includes representation and output demands rather than isolating one causal prompt ingredient. Localization, citation and request endpoints are not applicable to the direct arm.

Twelve source clauses, each from a different historical rule group, anchor the formal construction. They cover connectors, lifting capacity, motor power, single use, precious-metal plating, textile fraction, granule diameter, instrument mass, electrical power, frame species, propulsion power and fireplace mass. Each contributes two selected requirements and nine synthetic inputs. The complete original clause and selected quote spans remain in provenance. The controlled clause states the supplied interpretation explicitly; in particular, every-granule quantification and frame-specific teak scope are assumptions of the diagnostic, not validated legal readings of the full exclusion.

The nine variants comprise a complete base, deletion of the unique supporting fact, an irrelevant addition, a decisive contrary attribute, retention of that contradiction while another fact is removed, an object-binding original, its ownership swap, a semantic or unit equivalent and evidence about another product only. This yields 108 unique inputs, with 48 supported, 36 refuted and 24 unknown expected decisions. Eight linked relations per family yield 96 relations. Restoration reverses deletion using an already evaluated base endpoint; it is not an additional independent input. Source families share a construction template, so twelve families do not imply twelve independent legal populations.

The first condition carries the whole-input state changes in this construction. The second tests companion-state tracking and the suppression of requests after a decisive refutation; it is otherwise supported. Whole-input accuracy therefore cannot establish general integration of every necessary condition. The separate qualification set includes a supported first condition with an unknown second condition. The formal claim rests on the condition, evidence, request and paired-change endpoints actually exercised.

A pre-outcome input audit detected that short literal fragments left two interpretations more explicit in the checklist arm. The complete canonical clause was therefore supplied to both arms before formal outcomes were examined. Three responses from the superseded input pilot were preserved, excluded from formal analysis and not used to choose the model or prompt. The semantically aligned 108 inputs and protocol were hashed and frozen before the formal run. This was a documented input-semantic correction, rather than a claim of preregistration or a retrospective declaration that the collection was unseen by the researchers.

A separate semantic audit during inference identified an annotation defect in the frame-species family. In its refutation-with-missing variant, a teak-frame statement also supplies evidence that the frame is wooden. Removing a separate wooden-frame sentence therefore did not remove every support for that condition. The frozen inputs and predictions are preserved. An outcome-independent erratum changes that one condition state and permits either valid supporting fact, or both, for the seven affected citation endpoints. Corrected and frozen-reference scores are both retained, with a sensitivity excluding all nine inputs from that family. Overall reference decisions are unchanged. The decisive-refutation-with-another-unknown diagnostic has eleven valid families after this correction. This incident also illustrates why span matching alone cannot establish semantic label validity.

The real-source diagnostic is separate. Four directly recoverable conditions from three rulings are evaluated before and after adding their independently descriptive source statement, producing eight endpoints. One motor condition had already been inferred as supported in the historical coding; only the stricter direct-text policy withholds it before restoration. Thus four recoverable relations are not four newly corrected historical unknowns. These materials were identified through the source audit, so they are not an unseen customs generalization test. A condition can correctly change from unknown to supported while the whole case remains unresolved because another requirement needs interpretation.

### 4.3 Endpoints and interpretation

Decision agreement is accompanied by condition-state agreement, complete condition coverage, correct evidence-ID binding, missing-condition localization, request precision and recall, unsupported affirmative or refuting conditions and case conclusions, and unnecessary requests. A required gap omitted from the requested set counts as a substantive request failure even if every schema field is present. No request is necessary once a credible mandatory contradiction settles the conjunction. Unnecessary requests include asking for an established property or continuing after such a refutation.

Pair success requires both endpoints to be correct and to exhibit the specified relation. For an invariant addition or equivalent unit, two equally wrong answers fail. For deletion, restoration, decisive change or object swap, two identical unknown answers fail. We retain the model's overall decision separately from deterministic aggregation over its predicted conditions. This allows detection of a disagreement between condition reasoning and the final answer without hiding it behind an aggregator.

The short interface requires empty evidence for unknown. It consequently cannot encode partial support, competing factual claims or the richness of the source-audit cause fields. Contradictory source reports are a marked boundary of the ordinary constructed task, not a solved capability. The functional experiment concerns explicit controlled statements and supplied conjunctive requirements.

We report counts with their actual denominators and examine family-level patterns. We do not attach population confidence guarantees to the purpose-built templates or treat the 96 linked relations as independent trials. Historical resampling results describe variation within the selected corpus and do not correct unknown inclusion probabilities. Previously inspected numeric repairs remain development demonstrations; neither bootstrap resampling nor new random seeds can convert those same materials into independent validation.
