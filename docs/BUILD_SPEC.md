## Identity and main inference

Working title: Auditing evidence construction in legal AI evaluation

Target: Artificial Intelligence and Law, research article on legal AI evaluation and auditing. Use author–date citations, a nonstructured abstract, substantive methods/results, transparent data/AI disclosures and an anonymous main file. No journal acceptance probability is estimated.

The useful increment is the source-to-input evidence protocol. Three-valued entailment, evidence spans, symbolic aggregation, uncertainty and generic legal benchmark risks are established ideas and cannot be claimed as new. The article must demonstrate the increment through complete source traces and controlled diagnostics. Formal causal identification and prospective deployment claims are outside this question.

Primary questions:

1. Which evidence deficits in the frozen input are attributable to input selection, and which remain after independent attributes are traced in the complete public ruling?
2. Which condition-level representations expose interpretation and object/attribute/quantifier binding rather than silently converting them to labels?
3. In a qualified, frozen interface, can the proposed task endpoints distinguish correct missing-evidence behavior, invariance, decisive updates and contradiction preservation?

The direct layer is explicitly narrow. An exact span establishes provenance, not semantic validity. A supplied checklist is not an automatically extracted complete rule. Real public cases are purposive and retrospective. Constructed perturbations are specified tests of the protocol, not independent legal observations.

## Formal definitions

The article needs only the signed condition representation, three-valued mandatory-conjunction rule and metric definitions actually used. Do not add decorative formulas.

- c carries governed object, signed predicate, attribute, logical role, quantifier and measurement scope.
- e carries quote, exact full-source locator, source-statement class and admitted-input policy.
- S/R/U concern the stated textual evidence policy; conflicting or interpretive statements are not silently treated as direct truth.
- Under verified necessary conjuncts, any R gives R, all S gives S, otherwise U. Invalid output is separate; OR/exception structures require their own representation or remain out of scope for this aggregator.
- Request recall and unnecessary requests use explicit relevant missing-condition sets. No request is necessary once an independently verified mandatory contradiction settles the conjunction; conflicting evidence about the same predicate requires its own diagnostic.
- Invariance is credited together with both answers correct; a refusal on both sides of a decisive change is a failure.
- In the formal construction c1 carries the whole-input state changes. Companion c2 tracking and request suppression are separately scored; whole-input accuracy is not proof of general multi-condition integration.
- A pre-outcome frame-species reference erratum is applied without changing frozen inputs or predictions; original-reference and whole-family-exclusion sensitivities remain visible.

Hypothetical administrative-disagreement loss appears only in the supplement, with all assumptions and review-all comparison.
