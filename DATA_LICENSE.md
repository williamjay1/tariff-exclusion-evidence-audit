# Data rights and source attribution

This is not a blanket relicensing of upstream sources. Original software is
released under the MIT licence (`LICENSE`). Author-created condition records,
inventory readings, constructed diagnostic inputs, analysis outputs and figure
artwork are offered under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
to the extent of Junjie Zhang's rights. U.S. Government documents retain their
public-domain status. No endorsement by any agency is implied.

| Material | Version and use | Release decision and attribution |
|---|---|---|
| CBP customs rulings | 67 ruling snapshots used in the audit, plus the 2,482-document discovery corpus in `original_data/` | U.S. Government works, not subject to copyright in the United States (17 U.S.C. §105). Included as byte-identical copies for span verification. Retrieved from [CROSS](https://rulings.cbp.gov/). |
| USTR exclusion notices and index | Notice of 21 December 2018; index of 17 January 2024 used as structural context | U.S. Government works, public domain. The index is not the historical sampling frame; the article states this limit. |
| Author-created audit records | 208 condition records, 73 case records, 397 inventory rows, 1,096 coarse groups, interpretation options and policy packages | CC BY 4.0. Attribution: Junjie Zhang, *Tariff exclusion evidence audit*. |
| Constructed diagnostic inputs and saved model replies | 108 controlled inputs, 96 linked relations, 8 restoration endpoints, qualification and development runs | CC BY 4.0. Generated with the Qwen3-14B model identified in `inference_environment_manifest.json`; model weights are not redistributed. |
| Figures | Three figures as editable SVG, vector PDF and 1200 dpi PNG | CC BY 4.0. |
| Qwen3-14B weights | Not redistributed | Governed by the publisher's model licence; identifiers and hashes are recorded. |
| Third-party research papers | Bibliography metadata and short factual comparisons only | Not redistributed. Follow the DOI links in the manuscript references. |

Silence in a public ruling is not converted into a negative product attribute.
The audit distinguishes what the public record states from what a party asserts,
and it records that distinction rather than resolving it. No personal, private or
proprietary data are included; all sources are public federal documents.
