## One material annotation issue

**CF10_refute_missing, condition c2.** The sole fact is “The frame of Product A is made of teak.” The second requirement is “Product A must have a wooden frame.” The expected c2 state is unknown. Under ordinary lexical inclusion, teak is a wood, so the retained fact can establish c2. The same issue affects the uniqueness of the expected c2 evidence ID in base, equivalent, changed and binding variants: oak/teak frame facts are alternative evidence for wooden-frame composition. The protocol excludes likely properties and background expectations but does not formally prohibit ordinary taxonomic entailment. A unique-fact deletion claim is therefore not secure for this family.

The overall CF10_refute_missing decision remains refuted, and the request remains empty, under either reading. Consequently this is a condition/evidence-reference problem rather than a case-decision reversal. Preserve frozen inputs and predictions. A transparent annotation erratum and a stated condition/evidence sensitivity can handle it without modifying the running experiment. Do not choose the resolution from model performance. In manuscript Section 4.2, qualify “deletion of the unique supporting fact” for this identified material-hierarchy exception, and report the affected denominator in the condition/evidence results.
